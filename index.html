❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help users understand the monitoring dashboard.

What does the AQI value tell me?

AQI gives an overall indication of the air-quality condition at the monitored location. As the AQI increases, the level of pollution concern also increases.

Why are different areas assigned different zones?

Pollution conditions can vary from one monitored location to another. The system uses the available environmental readings to help classify areas into different pollution zones.

What is the purpose of AI Analysis?

AI Analysis converts numerical environmental readings into understandable information. Instead of looking only at numbers, users receive an interpretation of the current pollution condition.

Where does the monitoring data come from?

During the prototype stage, the dashboard can use sample sensor readings. During final integration, these values can be replaced with real-time data received from the backend and environmental sensors.

What should I do when pollution levels increase?

When pollution levels increase, the dashboard can highlight the condition through AI insights, recommendations and zone classification so that the monitored area can receive closer observation.
