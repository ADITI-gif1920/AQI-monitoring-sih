How Are Hotspots Identified?

The system uses environmental sensor readings to identify areas where pollution levels are increasing.

01

Sensor Data

Sensors installed around mining areas collect readings such as AQI, PM2.5, PM10 and gas levels.

02

Pollution Analysis

The collected readings are analysed to determine the current pollution condition of each monitored area.

03

Zone Classification

Areas are classified into Green, Yellow or Red zones according to their pollution levels.

04

Hotspot Detection

Locations showing higher pollution levels are highlighted on the monitoring map as potential pollution hotspots.
